Privacy Policy

How RPC Orbit collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our human resource management services.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Tejaswini Patil operates RPC Orbit (“the App”), a Human Resource Management System designed to help organizations manage employee information, attendance, leave requests, tasks, and related HR operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when employees, managers, HR personnel, and administrators use the App.

Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it separately. Use of the App does not replace any notice or consent required for specific data processing.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the features enabled by your organization and the way you use the App.

A. Employee information

  • Full name and employee ID
  • Work email address and phone number
  • Department, designation, and company information
  • Employment-related information provided by your employer
  • Profile photograph, where applicable

B. Account and authentication information

  • Login identifiers, authentication tokens, and account role
  • Access permissions and relevant security or login records

Passwords, where used, should be protected using appropriate password-hashing methods and should not be stored as plain text.

C. Attendance information

  • Check-in and check-out timestamps, attendance status, shifts, and working hours
  • Attendance correction requests and approval records
  • Attendance verification method and related audit records

D. Location information

If location-based attendance is enabled, the App may access device location to determine whether a check-in or check-out occurs within a designated workplace area. Depending on the configured method, this may include GPS coordinates or geofence results. If office-proximity attendance is enabled, the App may use Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to detect proximity to an authorized office device.

Location access is intended for the disclosed attendance purpose. The App should not perform continuous location tracking unless a separate, clearly disclosed and lawful feature requires it.

E. Face verification and liveness information

If face verification or liveness detection is enabled, the App may use the camera to capture an image for attendance verification. Depending on the technology, this may involve facial images, face templates or other biometric representations, and verification results.

This information is used to help verify employee identity and reduce fraudulent attendance. It may be processed by our systems or an authorized verification provider. We will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent or another applicable legal basis where required. Any alternative verification method will depend on the employer’s available process and applicable requirements.

F. Leave and task information

  • Leave applications, leave types, dates, reasons submitted, and approval status
  • Leave balances and manager comments
  • Task titles, descriptions, priorities, assignments, deadlines, and progress
  • Task attachments and related work records

G. Documents and attachments

If enabled, the App may process employee documents and files uploaded by authorized users for employment administration and related HR operations.

H. Technical and diagnostic information

Depending on the libraries and services integrated into the App, we may collect limited technical information such as device and operating system details, application logs, error reports, and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information, as appropriate, to:

  • Create and manage employee accounts
  • Provide HR, attendance, leave, and task management features
  • Record and verify attendance
  • Process leave requests and manager approvals
  • Manage employee documents and work-related attachments
  • Apply role-based access and company-level permissions
  • Maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and investigate misuse
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and improve service reliability
  • Meet applicable legal, employment, and recordkeeping obligations

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any required notice or consent.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared or made accessible when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Employers and authorized administrators: Authorized HR personnel, managers, and administrators may access information according to their roles and permissions.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: Providers may process information to host and operate the service, database, or file storage.
  • Verification service providers: If face matching or liveness detection uses a third-party service, relevant images or biometric representations may be sent to that provider as needed for verification.
  • Technical service providers: Authorized providers may support security, diagnostics, communications, or other necessary technical functions.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

We aim to limit sharing to information necessary for the relevant purpose. The final published policy should identify the actual providers used by the released App where required.

5. Data Storage and Security

The App’s backend and database may be hosted on cloud infrastructure, including Render, a Node.js service, and a MySQL database. The actual hosting and storage configuration should be confirmed before publication.

Appropriate safeguards should include:

  • HTTPS/TLS for data transmission
  • Authentication and role-based access controls
  • Restrictions on access to employee and company information
  • Secure handling of credentials, secrets, and authentication tokens
  • Database backups and recovery procedures
  • Appropriate safeguards for employee documents and biometric information
  • Security monitoring and maintenance

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We work to reduce security risks and protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet employment and business requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with applicable law. Retention periods may differ by data type.

Attendance or employment records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations. Temporary verification images should not be retained longer than necessary for the stated purpose unless there is a documented and lawful reason.

When information is no longer required, we will delete it or securely anonymize it, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Individuals may contact their employer or the privacy contact below to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Account deletion requests will be handled through the available process, subject to lawful retention requirements.

7. Employee Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and the individual’s relationship with their employer, individuals may be able to:

  • Request access to their personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Raise concerns or complaints about data processing

Requests involving employment records may need to be coordinated with the employer responsible for those records. Rights and procedures depend on applicable law.

8. Device Permissions

The App may request device permissions only for enabled features:

  • Camera: Face verification or other disclosed image-capture features.
  • Location: Location-based attendance, where enabled.
  • Bluetooth: Office-proximity attendance, where enabled.
  • Files or media: Selecting or uploading employee documents and task attachments, where enabled.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party infrastructure, verification services, or diagnostic tools. These providers may process information according to their roles and applicable contractual and privacy obligations. The final policy should identify the actual providers and link to their privacy information where appropriate.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for workplace and HR management use and is not intended to target children. If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published with an updated “Last updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide notice of material changes and obtain any necessary consent.
